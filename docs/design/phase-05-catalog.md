# Staff material catalog

Mode: Operate. Extend the approved account workspace with a staff material review and maintenance surface.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Staff can review a supplier candidate, confirm its specification and unit, then deliberately publish it.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Inter, indigo actions, paired neutral themes, quiet borders and the established Tailwind fields.

STORY: Enter from My account, search the material list, select a record, inspect its supplier source, edit details and add a new price before publishing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Heading and add action lead, followed by search and a material list beside the selected record. On narrow screens the record follows the list. No dashboard metrics or decorative cards.

FORM: Existing workspace extension; seed: none. Retirement requires an inline confirmation. Price history follows current editing controls.

FINISH: Verify the staff route, publishing boundary, preserved historical prices, responsive rendering and denied ordinary-user mutations before owner acceptance.

## Behavior

Catalog changes are serialized per material and reject stale versions. Edits and new prices unpublish the material. Imported supplier fields remain staff-only. Boards use sheet prices, edging uses meter prices, and publishing requires review plus a positive current price. Retirement retains details and prices. New prices are effective immediately; scheduling is outside this increment.
